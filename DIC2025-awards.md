## INTRODUCTION

The NYU Shanghai Digital Innovation Challenge (DIC) 2025 has successfully concluded, bringing together innovative teams from diverse backgrounds to showcase cutting-edge ideas under the theme of AI Agent Unlock Business Innovation. 

After rigorous evaluation by judges from academia and industry, outstanding teams were selected across both the Main Track and the Global Track. These awards recognize excellence in innovation, technical implementation, and real-world impact.

## MAIN TRACK AWARDS

The Main Track highlights top-performing teams across all competition tracks, recognizing both overall excellence and specialized strengths.

DIC 2025 showcased the power of interdisciplinary collaboration and the transformative potential of AI agents in solving real-world challenges. The awarded teams demonstrated exceptional creativity, technical strength, and a strong vision for impact.
