## INTRODUCTION

New York University Shanghai Digital Innovation Challenge (NYUSHDIC) is a tech-based innovation competition hosted by a student organization NYUSH Digital Innovation Community. Students from NYUSH and other universities are welcome to form cross-disciplinary teams, utilize cutting-edge digital technology, and pitch innovative ideas to address real-world problems. This year, the theme of the competition is AI Agent Unlock Business Innovation.

AI Agents, as one of the latest advances in artificial intelligence, are redefining how people interact with technology. They are intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, and act autonomously. Beyond executing human instructions, AI Agents are able to learn, adapt, and optimize, making them valuable tools for solving complex problems.

For this year’s challenge, under the theme AI Agent Unlock Business Innovation, participants will apply AI Agents to address practical business and social issues.

The competition will include several tracks composed of different fields, tentatively in the areas of **Education**, **Mental Wellbeing**, and **Finance**. Following the model from last year, each track will be supported by industry partners, faculty members or schools, who will set the problems, provide guidance, and conduct evaluations.

## Track information

#### 01 Education — Kiwi Track

**Mentor:** Prof. Hongyi Wen from NYUSH  

**Background:**  
Kiwi is a research platform developed at NYU Shanghai to support students and faculty in creating a seamless learning environment.  
The core of the Kiwi Track is to leverage the Kiwi Agent platform to provide intelligent solutions that enhance efficiency and learning outcomes in the educational process.  
In education, teachers face challenges such as the extensive time required for grading assignments and projects, and difficulties in ensuring objective and consistent scoring.  
Simultaneously, students often struggle to efficiently review and consolidate content from lengthy textbooks.  

This track aims to harness the natural language understanding capabilities and rule-application potential of AI Agents to develop two key educational support tools:  
- one for rapid, objective scoring and feedback to reduce teachers' repetitive labor;  
- another for automatically extracting key concepts from textbooks to generate interactive flashcards, thereby boosting students' review efficiency and self-directed learning.  

**The Key Goals:**  
- **Platform Integration:** Utilize the Kiwi Agent Platform to load course rubrics or the designated textbook as the system's database.  
- **Develop AI Grading System:** Design a system that generates a per-item score, rationale, and improvement suggestions for student submissions based on the rubric, providing formative feedback.  
- **Develop AI Flashcard System:** Create a system to automatically extract and generate accurate, interactive knowledge cards from the textbook, supporting categorization and self-test modes.  
- **Ensure Utility and Scalability:** The solution must be operational and support practical features like batch input/export or multi-modal content, ensuring its generality and efficiency.  
- **Maintain Fairness and Accuracy:** Address technical challenges to ensure AI scoring consistency and fairness and guarantee the accuracy of extracted knowledge points.

#### 02 Education — Mental Health Track: 
#### Building AI Agents for Student Motivation and Resilience

**Cooperative Enterprise:** 新通躺着学  

**Background:**  
This track invites participants to design AI Agents that support students’ psychological well-being and sustained learning motivation.  
While academic challenges often receive the most attention, many students struggle more with managing stress, anxiety, and self-expectations.  
The ability to stay motivated, resilient, and emotionally balanced is often what separates success from burnout.  

However, providing personalized emotional guidance and psychological support is labor-intensive and requires professional expertise.  
Most educational institutions lack such resources, resulting in teachers under heavy pressure and students learning under stress.  
This gap highlights a major opportunity for AI-driven solutions to complement human educators and improve overall learning outcomes.  

**The Key Goals:**  
- **Design an AI Agent for Mental Resilience:** Build an AI Agent (or a system of agents) that helps students manage stress, maintain motivation, and strengthen self-regulation during their learning journey.  
- **Integrate Educational and Psychological Insights:** Combine learning behavior data with basic psychological support frameworks to provide empathetic, adaptive guidance.  
- **Demonstrate Practical Use Cases:** Showcase how such agents can be deployed in schools, tutoring centers, or online learning platforms to support both students and teachers.  
- **Enable Sustainable Learning Outcomes:** Empower students to overcome emotional barriers, maintain consistent progress, and achieve better academic and personal growth.

#### 03 Healthcare Track
#### Designing AI Agents Across the Drug Development Life Cycle

**Cooperative Enterprise:** 复宏汉霖（复星集团）  

**Background:**  
The track challenges participants to build AI Agents to empower healthcare teams by efficiently gathering, synthesizing, and applying distributed knowledge throughout the entire drug life cycle.  

Developing a new drug is a lengthy, complex, and information-intensive process, from initial research to commercialization.  
Critical information—scientific breakthroughs, clinical trial progress, manufacturing standards, market intelligence, and internal operations—is scattered across various sources, including research papers, clinical databases, regulatory bodies, news sites, and internal knowledge bases.  
This fragmentation makes it difficult and time-consuming for healthcare professionals to access and act upon timely and accurate information, which is essential for success.  

**The Key Goals:**  
- **Design an Integrated AI Agent System:** Design an AI Agent (or system of agents) that integrates at least two of the five core domains of the drug development life cycle:  
  - Drug Literature & Pipeline Monitoring  
  - Clinical Trial Finder & Comparator  
  - Manufacturing & Quality Assurance Assistant  
  - Drug Licensing & Commercial Intelligence  
  - Pharma Operations Assistant  
- **Showcase Multi-Domain Value:** Demonstrate how combining multiple knowledge areas creates a more powerful and versatile digital assistant for healthcare teams.  
- **Incorporate Agent Capabilities:** Include monitoring, summarizing, comparing, Q&A support, and proactive alerting to help researchers, clinicians, quality/manufacturing teams, and business functions.  
- **Enable Faster, Better-Informed Decisions:** Provide timely, accurate, and actionable information to support Henlius teams in making faster and better-informed decisions across functions.

#### 04 Finance Track
#### AI Agents Unlock Finance for Sustainability

**Cooperator:** Duke Kunshan University  

**Background:**  
The Finance Track of the 2025 Digital Innovation Challenge focuses on the intersection of AI agents, FinTech, and sustainability.  
AI agents, as adaptive decision-making systems, are transforming financial services, making them more efficient, transparent, and accessible.  
When combined with FinTech, these technologies can promote global sustainability by enabling green investment allocation, supporting carbon accounting, and aligning financial behavior with environmental goals.  
The track aims to inspire solutions that advance both financial services and sustainable development.  

**The Key Goals:**  
- **Design Innovative Solutions:** Task participants with providing innovative, ethical, and sustainable financial solutions.  
- **Harness AI for Sustainability:** Demonstrate how AI agents can be used to unlock finance as a force for long-term sustainability and equitable progress.  
- **Focus on Key Technologies:** Encourage exploration of technologies like blockchain, carbon accounting, and credit scoring systems for sustainable finance development.  
- **Prioritize Foundational Values:** Value solutions based on Frontier Agents (autonomy, adaptability, real-world integration), Openness, Ethics & Inclusion, and clear Impact on Sustainable Development Goals.  
- **Produce Tangible Projects:** Guide teams to complete tangible projects, supported by data and feasibility analysis, with potential for real-world implementation.
